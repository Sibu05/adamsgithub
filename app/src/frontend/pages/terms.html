1. Acceptance of these terms

By creating an account or using Adamas2Aurum you agree to these Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy below. If you do not agree, do not create an account or use the game. We record which version of these terms you accepted and when; when the terms change materially we will ask you to accept the new version before you continue playing.

2. Eligibility

Adamas2Aurum is built for the University of the Witwatersrand community and is intended for users aged 18 and older. If you are under 18, you may only use the game with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian.

3. Accounts & sign-in

You may sign in with Google OAuth or with a username and PIN. You are responsible for keeping your PIN confidential and for all activity under your account. Usernames may not contain “@” so that nobody can impersonate another person's email address. One account per person: do not create duplicate accounts to gain an advantage. Tell us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

4. Location data

Adamas2Aurum is a location-based game. This is how your location is handled:

  • Your live position is read by your own browser to show your avatar on the map. Simply moving around the map does not send your position to our servers.
  • When you attempt a challenge, your claimed position, the computed distance to the event, your travel speed between checks, and the verification outcome are recorded so the game can confirm you are really there and detect spoofed locations.
  • Location checks happen only while you are using the game. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Some campus areas may have poor GPS reception; the game may offer a demo or QR fallback where a GPS fix cannot be verified.

5. Gameplay & content you create

Your trivia attempts, answers, card collection, decks, battles, trades, points, and feedback reports are stored so the game functions and so progress is kept between sessions. Your display name and points may appear on public leaderboards. By submitting feedback or other content you confirm it is yours to share and you give us permission to use it to operate and improve the game.

6. Fair play & moderation

Location spoofing, GPS manipulation, exploiting bugs, harassing other players, or submitting false reports may lead to graduated sanctions: warning, restriction, or suspension, applied by moderators. Automated trust scoring helps surface suspicious play for human review; sanctions are decided by the moderation team, and you may appeal by contacting us (see §17).

7. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • Break the law or infringe anyone's rights while playing, including trespassing or entering restricted campus areas to reach a stop.
  • Attempt to access other players' accounts, probe or disrupt our servers, or circumvent access controls and rate limits.
  • Use bots, scripts, or modified clients to play on your behalf.
  • Upload harmful, hateful, or unlawful content through feedback or any other channel.

Always stay aware of your surroundings while playing. Do not play while driving, cycling, or in any situation where distraction puts you or others at risk.

8. Privacy: what we collect and why

We process your personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), only for the purposes below:

  • Account information — display name, username, email address, and avatar. Purpose: identify your account and display your profile.
  • Sign-in credentials — your PIN is stored only as a salted, one-way scrypt hash; we cannot read it back. Google sign-in is handled by Google OAuth; we receive only the profile details needed to link your account.
  • Sessions — a secure, HttpOnly session cookie keeps you signed in for up to 24 hours.
  • Location checks — per challenge attempt as described in §4. Purpose: verifying presence and preventing cheating.
  • Gameplay records — attempts, cards, battles, trades, points, and feedback reports. Purpose: operating the game and leaderboards.
  • Moderation records — trust scores, evidence, and sanctions. Purpose: fair play enforcement.
  • Terms acceptances — which version you accepted and when. Purpose: proving consent.

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with service providers strictly necessary to run the game (hosting infrastructure and Google for sign-in), and with authorities where required by law. Map tiles and libraries load from third-party content networks, which technically receive your IP address like any website you visit.

9. Your consent (POPIA section 11)

POPIA requires your consent to be voluntary, specific, and informed. When you tick the acceptance box (or tap Accept in the app), you consent to each of the following, and nothing else:

  • Account operation — creating and maintaining your profile, credentials, and session so you can sign in and play.
  • Location verification — checking your claimed position when you attempt challenges and keeping those verification logs to prevent cheating, as described in §4.
  • Gameplay and leaderboards — recording your progress and showing your display name and points publicly, as described in §5.
  • Fair-play review — scoring suspicious play and applying graduated sanctions, as described in §6.
  • Support inbox — using your feedback reports and contacting you about them.

You may withdraw consent at any time by requesting account deletion (see §14). Withdrawing consent ends your ability to play, because the game cannot function without this processing. Withdrawing consent does not retroactively make past lawful processing unlawful.

10. How we meet POPIA's eight conditions

POPIA (sections 8–25) sets eight conditions for lawful processing. This is how each one applies here — the Adamas2Aurum team is the “responsible party” for your information:

  • Accountability (§8) — we take responsibility for lawful processing and these public terms are part of that.
  • Processing limitation (§§9–12) — we process information lawfully, collect directly from you where possible, and collect no more than §8 lists. Location is read on your device and sent to our servers only when needed for verification.
  • Purpose specification (§§13–14) — every item in §8 is collected for a stated purpose, and we keep it no longer than needed: sessions expire after 24 hours; account, gameplay, location check, and moderation records are kept while your account is active and removed on deletion (see §14); records of which terms version you accepted are kept afterwards as proof of consent.
  • Further processing limitation (§15) — we do not reuse your information for new purposes (such as research or advertising) without fresh consent or another lawful ground.
  • Information quality (§16) — we rely on the details you supply and correct them on request (see §14).
  • Openness (§§17–18) — this document is our notification to you of what is collected, why, and who to contact.
  • Security safeguards (§§19–22) — see §15. If we ever suspect a breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.
  • Your participation (§§23–25) — see §14 for access, correction, and deletion.

11. Children's information

POPIA (sections 34–35) gives extra protection to the personal information of children under 18, which may only be processed with the consent of a competent person (parent or guardian). Consistent with §2, players under 18 may use Adamas2Aurum only with a parent or guardian's permission, and by accepting these terms on a child's behalf that competent person consents to the processing described here. We do not knowingly collect information from children without that consent; if you believe a child is playing without it, contact us (see §17) and we will remove the account.

12. Transfers outside South Africa

Some processing happens outside South Africa, which POPIA (section 72) permits on the basis of your consent in §9 and, where applicable, contracts with our providers:

  • Google sign-in — authentication is handled by Google on its own global infrastructure under Google's privacy policy.
  • Hosting and content delivery — game servers, map tiles, and app libraries are served from third-party infrastructure and content networks that may sit outside the country.

Your information is never sold or transferred to unrelated third parties.

13. Direct marketing

We do not send advertising or direct marketing (POPIA section 69). Any messages we send relate to operating your account, the game, or support requests you raised.

14. Your rights over your information

Under POPIA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may object to certain processing or withdraw consent (which ends your ability to play, see §9). To exercise these rights, contact us (see §17) from your account email address.

If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za), which enforces POPIA.

Please note the current limits of self-service: in-app username editing and account deletion are not yet available in the app itself, so account deletion is handled manually by an administrator on request. Deleting your account removes your profile, credentials, gameplay records, and feedback; anonymised aggregates (such as leaderboard history snapshots) and records we must keep by law may be retained.

15. Security

We protect your information with measures including salted one-way hashing of PINs, HttpOnly session cookies, login attempt rate limiting, and role-based access controls on the author console. No system is perfectly secure, so protect your PIN and sign out on shared devices.
